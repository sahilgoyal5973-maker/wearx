Crafting Clothes with Soul & Substance

Born out of a small design studio in Southwest Delhi, WearX was created with a clear philosophy: you shouldn't have to choose between luxury aesthetics and honest craftsmanship.

Our Origins

From Delhi's Streets to Wardrobes Pan-India

In 2022, tired of synthetic fast-fashion brands flooding the market with cheap polyesters and generic cuts, our founders set out to build an alternative right here in Southwest Delhi. We wanted clothing with weight, texture, and character.

We took our cue from organic earth tones — the sunbaked terracotta of Delhi's pottery hubs, the muted olive leaves of Aravalli ridges, and the serene sandstone beige of Delhi's historic monuments. Today, WearX serves over 10,000 customers with everyday casuals, festive ethnics, performance sportswear, nightlife party outfits, and cozy winter outerwear.

Our Philosophy

The 3 Pillars of WearX

What goes into every single garment we cut, sew, and ship.

1

Pure & Breathable Fabrics

We say no to plastic-heavy polyesters. Our tees, kurtas, and trousers are cut from combed cotton, pure linen, and certified non-toxic dyes that breathe with your skin.

2

Local Artisan Empowerment

Every piece is tailored by local master karigars in Delhi NCR. We pay fair wages, maintain humane workshop hours, and preserve traditional embroidery crafts like Lucknowi Chikankari.

3

The 3-Click Shopping Rule

No endless checkout mazes or forced app downloads. We designed WearX so any customer can discover, inspect, and order their exact size in under 3 clicks.
